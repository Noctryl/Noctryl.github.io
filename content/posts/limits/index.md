---
title: "Limits."
date: 2025-11-20T12:35:00+05:30
tags: ["Retrospection"]
---

I keep telling myself the same bloody thing every day - “I’ll study today. I swear. Today I’m finally starting.”
And then I don’t.
And the stupid part?
I actually believe it every morning.
Like an idiot with amnesia repeating the same hopeful script that never plays out.

Two, three weeks of this. or maybe months
Every day unrolling the same lie and wrapping myself in it like a blanket I know is full of holes.
It’s almost ridiculous.
Almost funny if it didn’t hurt so much.

And the worst part is - I don’t even know where the day goes.
I blink and it’s evening.
Blink again and somehow it’s night.
And the guilt comes crawling back, sitting on my chest like it owns the place.
“You wasted another one. Brilliant. Well done, lad.”

I try to argue with it but the voice in my head is quicker.
Sharper.
It doesn’t even yell - it just throws the truth back at me in that quiet tone that slices deeper than shouting ever could.

All of this was already simmering under the surface, and then my mum talked about my marks with my masi and something inside me just… tilted.
Not a big collapse - just a subtle shift, like the floor moved half an inch and suddenly I couldn’t stand straight anymore.

They weren’t even angry.
Just disappointed.
Worried.
Soft voices, careful words - which somehow make it ten times worse because I don’t have the strength to deal with gentle concern.
I’d take shouting over that any day.
At least shouting gives you something to push back against.

But this?
This just made every broken promise echo inside my head like a room full of mirrors showing me all the versions of myself I said I would be by now.

And then i found my vision blurry.
I don’t even know what exactly cracked me today.
It was tiny.
A hairline fracture in a glass that was already strained.

I kept thinking, I need a break.
A proper one where my brain stops buzzing and my chest stops feeling tight.
But then this panic kicked in -
You can’t take a break, you’re already drowning. Who pauses mid-drown?

And that’s where the chaos starts, right?
Because I’m stuck between two parts of myself -
the one that’s screaming for rest
and the one that’s screaming about the consequences of taking it.

Both screaming.
Neither listening.

I’m tired of this loop.
Tired of hearing myself promise things I know I won’t do.
Tired of pretending tomorrow will magically turn me into someone disciplined, someone focused, someone who doesn’t keep tripping over the same bloody routine.

But weirdly…
even after all that,
some tiny part of me still whispers,
Maybe tomorrow. Maybe tomorrow you’ll actually start, mate.
And I hate that I still believe it.
Just a little.
Just enough to wake up and try again.
Or at least pretend I will.

Right now everything feels loud and quiet at the same time -
like my mind is a room full of knocked-over chairs and tangled wires,
and I’m standing in the middle trying to decide whether to fix it
or just sit on the floor for a bit
and breathe.
